About This View

This visualization organizes the Fluxus collection by medium, blended with Mieko Shiomi's "Disappearing Music for Face" (1966), a Fluxus film that captures a smile slowly fading to a neutral expression. The collection is divided into six quadrants: painting, drawing, print, sculpture, photograph, and mixed media.
