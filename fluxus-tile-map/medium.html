How to Use the Fluxus Medium Mosaic

This interactive visualization displays artworks from the Fluxus movement organized by artistic medium.

Navigation:

  • Pan: Click and drag to move around the mosaic
  • Zoom: Use mouse wheel to zoom in/out
  • Select Image: Click on any artwork to view its details
  • Reset View: Click the Reset View button to return to the default view

Medium Filters:

  • All: View all mediums
  • Painting: Oil, acrylic, and other paint mediums
  • Drawing: Pencil, ink, charcoal works
  • Print: Lithographs, etchings, and other print works
  • Sculpture: Three-dimensional works
  • Photography: Photos, films, and video documentation
  • Mixed Media: Books, collages, and combined techniques
