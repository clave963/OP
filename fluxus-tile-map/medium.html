About This View

This visualization organizes the Fluxus collection by medium, blended with Mieko Shiomi's "Disappearing Music for Face" (1966). The collection is divided into six categories: painting, drawing, print, sculpture, photograph, and mixed media.

How to Use the Medium Explorer

This interactive visualization organizes Fluxus artworks by medium type, arranged in a grid pattern that reveals how different materials and techniques were used throughout the movement.

Navigation

  • Pan: Click and drag to move around the mosaic
  • Zoom: Use mouse wheel to zoom in and out
  • Select Image: Click on any artwork to view its details
  • Reset View: Click the Reset View button or press R key
  • Toggle UI: Press F key to hide/show all UI elements

Medium Categories

  • Painting: Oil, acrylic, and other painted works
  • Drawing: Pencil, ink, charcoal, and drawn works
  • Print: Lithographs, screenprints, and printed multiples
  • Sculpture: Three-dimensional works and installations
  • Photograph: Photographic documentation and art
  • Mixed Media: Works combining multiple techniques

Controls

  • Grid Lines: Toggle visibility of category boundaries
  • Category Labels: Show/hide medium type labels
  • Hero Image: Toggle the underlying hero image effect
