About the Hero Image

This mosaic is arranged over Mieko Shiomi's "Disappearing Music for Face" (1966), a Fluxus film study of a smile slowly transforming into a neutral expression. The work explores the subtle transitions of facial expression over time, reflecting Fluxus interests in everyday gestures and time-based art.

How to Use the Fluxus Art Mosaic

This interactive visualization displays artworks from the Fluxus movement (1953-1984), arranged chronologically from bottom to top.

Navigation

  • Pan: Click and drag to move around the mosaic
  • Zoom: Use mouse wheel to zoom in and out
  • Select Image: Click on any artwork to view its details
  • Reset View: Click the Reset View button or press R key to return to the default view
  • Toggle UI: Press F key to hide/show all UI elements

Features

  • Year Ruler: The vertical timeline on the left shows the chronological progression of Fluxus art
  • Era Filters: Use the buttons in the sidebar to highlight specific Fluxus eras
  • Image Details: Click any artwork to view its metadata including artist, title, date and medium
  • Save View: Use the Save Current View button to capture the current visualization as a PNG image
  • View by Medium: Switch to the medium-based organization to explore artworks by material type

The colors of the artworks correspond to their era in the Fluxus movement:

  • Early Fluxus (1953-1963): Grayscale tones
  • Core Fluxus (1964-1973): Sepia tones
  • Late Fluxus (1974-1984): Colorized with red enhancement
