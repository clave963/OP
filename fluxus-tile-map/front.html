How to Use the Fluxus Art Mosaic

This interactive visualization displays artworks from the Fluxus movement (1953-1984), arranged chronologically from bottom to top.

Navigation:

  • Pan: Click and drag to move around the mosaic
  • Zoom: Use mouse wheel, or the Zoom buttons
  • Select Image: Click on any artwork to view its details
  • Reset View: Click the Reset View button to return to the default view

Features:

  • Year Ruler: The vertical timeline on the left shows the chronological progression of Fluxus art
  • Era Filters: Use the buttons at the bottom to highlight specific Fluxus eras
  • Image Details: Click any artwork to view its metadata including artist, title, date and medium

The colors of the artworks correspond to their era in the Fluxus movement:

  • Early Fluxus (1953-1963): Grayscale tones
  • Core Fluxus (1964-1973): Sepia tones
  • Late Fluxus (1974-1984): Colorized with red enhancement
